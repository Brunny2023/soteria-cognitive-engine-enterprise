# ADR 0002: Preserve Lovable Ownership and Tracking History

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-19

## Decision

This upgrade preserves Lovable-specific files, references, ownership/attribution obligations, and published tracking history. It does not rewrite history, remove Lovable fingerprints, or imply that generated material is independently owned. Future removal or replacement of Lovable dependencies requires a separately approved provenance and licensing workstream.

## Rationale

The current repository is connected to Lovable and its published history must remain intact. The immediate goal is to improve reproducibility, security evidence, ownership, and release discipline without changing the platform’s historical rights position.
