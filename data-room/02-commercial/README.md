# 02 — Commercial

## Revenue model

The repository does not contain verified revenue, contracts, customers, retention, or margin records. The plausible commercial model is B2B software with a combination of:

- Annual platform subscription by organization or deployment.
- Usage or model-infrastructure pass-through for AI requests.
- Paid implementation, connector, policy, and workflow configuration.
- Optional support, security review, and managed operations.

These are transaction hypotheses, not historical financial results.

## Target customers

Potential customer profiles include:

- Mid-market and enterprise organizations with governed knowledge and decision workflows.
- Professional-services teams that need repeatable domain-specialist workflows.
- Regulated or audit-sensitive teams that require approvals, evidence, and retention controls.
- Technology groups seeking a customizable internal cognition or agent-orchestration platform.

Buyer diligence should validate willingness to pay through customer interviews, pilots, pipeline evidence, and measurable workflow outcomes.

## TAM / SAM / SOM analysis

A defensible market model is not included because no external market dataset or customer segmentation study was supplied. The buyer should build:

- **TAM:** organizations with governed knowledge, decision, and workflow needs.
- **SAM:** target geographies, industries, deployment models, and data environments served by the selected product scope.
- **SOM:** reachable accounts based on sales capacity, integrations, implementation bandwidth, and compliance requirements.

Required inputs: account universe, ACV bands, implementation conversion, sales cycle, churn, gross margin, model cost, hosting cost, and support ratio.

## Competitive landscape

The product sits conceptually between enterprise knowledge platforms, workflow/orchestration tools, AI agent platforms, decision-intelligence products, and professional-services automation. A buyer should compare:

- Governance and human-approval depth
- Tenant isolation and deployment control
- Knowledge ingestion and graph capabilities
- Model routing and evaluation discipline
- Auditability and retention controls
- Time to deploy and integration burden

No competitor market-share or superiority claim is made here.

## Revenue potential model

Use this transparent model in the restricted financial workpaper:

```text
ARR = organizations × annual platform fee
    + usage revenue
    + implementation revenue
    + support revenue

Gross margin = (ARR - model costs - hosting - support delivery) / ARR
Net revenue retention = (opening ARR + expansion - contraction - churn) / opening ARR
```

Populate only with source-backed assumptions and label forecast, pipeline, pilot, and realized revenue separately.

## Go-to-market opportunity

A credible initial motion is a narrow reference workflow: choose one high-value, audit-sensitive workflow; connect a bounded knowledge set; define approval and evaluation metrics; measure time saved, decision quality, exception rate, and cost per completed outcome; then expand by department or workflow family.
