# 02 — Commercial

## Commercial asset thesis

Coxec's commercial value is an **asset-value thesis, not a revenue forecast**. The repository contains an implemented enterprise software foundation that may help a buyer accelerate its own product, internal platform, enterprise deployment, or strategic integration. The relevant value drivers are the implemented architecture, governed execution model, reusable AI and execution infrastructure, security and authorization design, technical documentation, testing and release system, and preserved implementation provenance.

This thesis does not assert revenue, customers, contracts, retention, margins, TAM, ARR, market share, production scale, or superiority over a named competitor. Those matters remain buyer diligence items.

## Revenue model hypotheses

The repository does not contain verified revenue, contracts, customers, retention, or margin records. Plausible future commercial models include:

- Annual platform subscription by organization or deployment.
- Usage or model-infrastructure pass-through for AI requests.
- Paid implementation, connector, policy, and workflow configuration.
- Optional support, security review, and managed operations.

These are transaction hypotheses, not historical financial results.

## Target customers and use cases

Potential customer profiles include:

- Mid-market and enterprise organizations with governed knowledge and decision workflows.
- Professional-services teams that need repeatable domain-specialist workflows.
- Regulated or audit-sensitive teams that require approvals, evidence, and retention controls.
- Technology groups seeking a customizable internal cognition or agent-orchestration platform.

Buyer diligence should validate willingness to pay through customer interviews, pilots, pipeline evidence, and measurable workflow outcomes.

## TAM / SAM / SOM analysis

A defensible market model is not included because no external market dataset or customer segmentation study was supplied. The buyer should build:

- **TAM:** organizations with governed knowledge, decision, and workflow needs.
- **SAM:** target geographies, industries, deployment models, and data environments served by the selected product scope.
- **SOM:** reachable accounts based on sales capacity, integrations, implementation bandwidth, and compliance requirements.

Required inputs include account universe, ACV bands, implementation conversion, sales cycle, churn, gross margin, model cost, hosting cost, and support ratio.

## Competitive landscape

The product sits conceptually between enterprise knowledge platforms, workflow and orchestration tools, AI agent platforms, decision-intelligence products, and professional-services automation. No competitor market-share or superiority claim is made here.

A buyer assessing build-versus-buy should separately account for the engineering categories it would otherwise need to assemble: domain workflow modeling; organization, role, and authorization boundaries; governed execution and human approval; validation and artifact handling; AI gateway and provider abstraction; audit-oriented records; deployment and infrastructure templates; security documentation; automated release gates; dependency and SBOM evidence; and operational runbooks. This is a description of asset scope, not a ranking.

## Transparent revenue-potential workpaper

Use the following model only in a restricted financial workpaper populated with source-backed assumptions:

```text
ARR = organizations × annual platform fee
    + usage revenue
    + implementation revenue
    + support revenue

Gross margin = (ARR - model costs - hosting - support delivery) / ARR
Net revenue retention = (opening ARR + expansion - contraction - churn) / opening ARR
```

Label forecast, pipeline, pilot, and realized revenue separately. No values are asserted in this public technical data room.

## Go-to-market opportunity

A credible initial motion is a narrow reference workflow: choose one high-value, audit-sensitive workflow; connect a bounded knowledge set; define approval and evaluation metrics; measure time saved, decision quality, exception rate, and cost per completed outcome; then expand by department or workflow family.
