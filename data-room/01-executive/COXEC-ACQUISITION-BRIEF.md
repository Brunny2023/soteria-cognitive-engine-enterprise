# Coxec — Enterprise Software Asset Acquisition Brief

## Opportunity

**Coxec is an enterprise software platform and associated IP, documentation and deployment assets available for acquisition.**

The proposed asset is a technical foundation for governed organizational workflows, validation, artifacts, authorization, and AI-assisted execution. It is not presented as a revenue-generating SaaS business.

## What the buyer is acquiring

Subject to definitive agreement, chain-of-title verification, and third-party transfer restrictions, the Coxec asset may include:

- Enterprise application architecture and domain workflow implementation.
- Governed execution, validation, approval, and artifact workflows.
- Organization and membership architecture with role and authorization concepts.
- AI gateway and provider abstraction interfaces.
- Security, authorization, RLS, input-validation, and audit-oriented design.
- Release engineering, automated testing, dependency scanning, and SBOM generation.
- Deployment and infrastructure templates.
- Technical, security, operations, and product documentation.
- Reproducible validation and release process.
- Coxec-specific IP and provenance materials, subject to chain-of-title confirmation.
- Coxec domain or brand rights only if separately designated in the definitive agreement.
- Expressly agreed transition assistance.

## Buyer value proposition

The economic proposition is to **acquire an existing enterprise software foundation rather than build the foundation from zero**. The acquisition is intended for a buyer seeking to accelerate an enterprise software, AI workflow, governed execution, or organizational intelligence initiative without building the underlying platform foundation from zero. Potential buyer value may include:

- Engineering time saved by starting from an existing architecture.
- A concrete architectural starting point for a buyer’s own product or internal platform.
- Reduced initial development risk compared with an unstructured greenfield effort.
- Reusable enterprise workflow, validation, authorization, and execution infrastructure.
- Reusable AI gateway and provider-integration boundaries.
- Technical, security, and operations documentation already organized for diligence.
- Automated testing, release gates, dependency evidence, and reproducible build procedures.
- Ability to customize the foundation for a buyer’s vertical, data estate, governance model, or enterprise use case.

These benefits are qualitative planning considerations. No unsupported dollar savings, revenue, ARR, customer, market-size, scale, certification, uptime, or AI-accuracy claim is made.

## Evidence posture

The repository and data room distinguish implemented and locally evidenced behavior from environment-dependent or outstanding items. The authoritative transaction release is recorded in [DATA-ROOM-INDEX.md](../../DATA-ROOM-INDEX.md).

Verified or evidenced materials include the local build and release gate, automated tests, static security contracts, RLS migration coverage, sensitive-file checks, dependency audit, SBOM generation, public/auth configuration-safe behavior, and product documentation. Live Supabase authentication, tenant isolation, provider workflows, backup/restore, production operations, and independent security review remain environment-dependent or outstanding.

## Possible transaction structures

1. **Outright asset/IP acquisition:** agreed Coxec source, agreed Coxec IP, documentation, deployment and release assets, optional expressly listed domain/brand rights, and agreed transition assistance.
2. **Exclusive commercial license:** exclusive commercial rights within a defined field and territory while the seller retains underlying ownership unless otherwise agreed.
3. **Strategic or non-exclusive license:** rights for integration or internal deployment, with implementation, support, usage, and provider terms separately negotiated.

No structure transfers Soteria corporate entities, unrelated products or IP, unrelated repositories or domains, corporate accounts, customer data, credentials, third-party accounts, non-transferable rights, or seller liabilities unless expressly agreed and legally transferable.

## Buyer diligence priorities

A serious buyer should validate chain of title, contributor and third-party rights, historical credential remediation, dependency and model-provider terms, domain/brand ownership, staging deployment, live authorization and tenant isolation, provider failure behavior, backup/restore, rollback, and support/transition scope before closing.
