# 08 — Transaction

## Preferred transaction structure

The preferred structure for a Coxec sale is an **outright asset acquisition and transfer of agreed commercial rights**, subject to legal review, confirmed chain of title, third-party rights clearance, and a definitive agreement.

An **exclusive license** is the principal alternative. A non-exclusive commercial license and a strategic integration are secondary alternatives where an outright transfer or exclusivity is not appropriate.

No structure transfers Soteria AI Technologies Limited, Soteria AI Technologies Inc., unrelated Soteria products, unrelated repositories, unrelated intellectual property, corporate accounts, or unrelated domains unless those items are expressly identified in a separate definitive agreement.

## Buyer receives

Subject to the definitive agreement, confirmed chain of title, and applicable legal and third-party transfer restrictions, the buyer will receive the expressly agreed Coxec assets listed below:

- The agreed Coxec source repository or release archive at the agreed transaction commit.
- Source code, migrations, tests, and configuration examples.
- Deployment and infrastructure templates.
- Release workflows and the reproducible validation process.
- Technical, security, operations, and architecture documentation.
- Product evidence included in the agreed data-room package.
- SBOM and dependency materials.
- Agreed Coxec-specific IP and provenance materials, subject to chain-of-title confirmation.
- Agreed Coxec domain or brand rights only if expressly listed in the definitive agreement.
- Agreed transition assistance under a transaction schedule or separate statement of work.

## Buyer does not receive by default

The following are excluded unless expressly assigned in the definitive agreement:

- Soteria AI Technologies Limited or Soteria AI Technologies Inc.
- Unrelated Soteria products, intellectual property, repositories, or domains.
- Customer, personal, confidential, or private data.
- Credentials, secrets, tokens, API keys, provider configurations, or private keys.
- Third-party provider, hosting, cloud, email, monitoring, or registrar accounts.
- Trademark ownership or corporate brand rights not specifically listed.
- Undisclosed liabilities, warranties, or external legal representation.
- Guarantees of revenue, customer adoption, certification, uptime, production scale, or AI performance.
- Any long-term support obligation not stated in a signed schedule or statement of work.

## License and rights alternatives

| Priority | Structure | Buyer receives | Key negotiation points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preferred | Outright asset acquisition / transfer | Agreed Coxec source, documentation, and transferable commercial rights | Chain of title, exclusions, representations, escrow, liabilities, domain/brand scope |
| Principal alternative | Exclusive license | Exclusive commercial use within a defined scope | Reserved rights, field, territory, term, enforcement, minimum commitments |
| Secondary alternative | Non-exclusive commercial license | Defined use, deployment, and modification rights | Field, territory, term, support, sublicensing |
| Secondary alternative | Strategic integration | Limited code and integration rights | Confidentiality, roadmap, services, ownership of improvements |

Counsel must convert the selected structure into a signed agreement.

## Asset transfer sequence

Recommended sequence:

1. Diligence access and buyer technical validation.
2. Rights, provenance, dependency, model, media, domain, and trademark clearance.
3. Agreement of the exact asset perimeter and transaction release identity.
4. Signed definitive agreement, including consideration, escrow, indemnity, and liabilities.
5. Credential remediation and written confirmation that historical and current credentials are rotated, revoked, or inactive.
6. Repository/archive transfer and any expressly included domain or brand transfer.
7. Buyer-controlled staging deployment and completion of the blocked security and environment-dependent tests.
8. Transition acceptance and optional support handoff.

## Credential remediation closing prerequisite

Before repository transfer, every credential, token, API key, provider configuration, or secret discovered in repository history or current files must be rotated, revoked, removed from active use, or otherwise confirmed inactive. The buyer and seller should retain machine-verifiable evidence of the remediation and record any residual historical exposure in the definitive agreement.

## Domain and brand separation

Coxec-specific domain and brand rights are separate from ownership of Soteria corporate entities and unrelated Soteria intellectual property. Transfer of any Coxec domain, trademark, logo, or brand asset occurs only when that asset is expressly listed in the definitive agreement and its ownership is confirmed.

## Transition and support

Support is optional and separately scoped. Possible handoff packages include documentation-only transfer, a fixed transition period, architecture walkthroughs, deployment assistance, issue triage, or paid implementation. No implied long-term support, SLA, uptime commitment, or maintenance obligation is created by this data room. Any transition assistance must be defined by a separate statement of work or explicit transaction schedule.

## Consideration and pricing

No sale price, ARR, valuation, or revenue forecast is inserted into this public technical data room. Consideration depends on the rights granted, asset perimeter, exclusivity, transfer scope, support and transition obligations, liabilities, escrow, representations, and other negotiated terms.

## Closing checklist

- Confirm the exact transaction release commit and associated hosted CI/evidence package.
- Confirm included and excluded files, rights, accounts, domains, and brand assets.
- Complete IP assignments and contributor declarations.
- Rotate, revoke, or confirm inactive all historical and current credentials.
- Complete dependency, model, media, domain, trademark, and third-party rights review.
- Agree consideration, license or acquisition terms, escrow, indemnity, liabilities, and support schedule.
- Execute buyer staging deployment and the blocked security and environment-dependent tests.
- Record acceptance evidence, transfer authority, and any post-closing transition obligations.
