# 07 — IP & Legal

## IP ownership

The repository contains a commercial license notice and buyer license schedule, but a transaction still requires signed chain-of-title evidence. Counsel should confirm ownership of source, documentation, media, trademarks, domain, configuration, and any contributed work.

## Contributor provenance

Historical source provenance is intentionally preserved. The [IP/provenance dossier](../../docs/legal/IP-PROVENANCE-DOSSIER.md) identifies assignments, contributor declarations, platform-rights confirmations, and unresolved questions without placing confidential evidence in the repository.

## Third-party licenses

Review `package.json`, the lockfile, the generated SBOM, and [the third-party schedule](../../docs/legal/third-party-and-provenance.md). Buyer counsel should confirm package licenses, model/provider terms, media rights, fonts, icons, hosting terms, and any copyleft or notice obligations.

## Dependency schedule

Regenerate `reports/sbom.cdx.json` for the transferred revision. Attach a human-reviewed schedule that distinguishes runtime dependencies, build dependencies, optional providers, and buyer-operated services.

## Domain ownership

The live site is observed at [coxec.soteriatech.pro](https://coxec.soteriatech.pro). Domain registration, DNS, certificate, mailbox, analytics, trademark, and transfer rights are not proven by this repository and must be documented in restricted diligence materials.

## Trademark and brand assets

The repository uses Soteria SECP product branding. Trademark clearance, logo rights, font/media licenses, and post-transfer brand permissions require owner and counsel confirmation.

## Buyer license schedule

See [buyer license schedule](../../docs/legal/buyer-license-schedule.md). It is a diligence checklist, not a signed transfer agreement or legal opinion.
